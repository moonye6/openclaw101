# What 81,000 People Really Want from AI: Key Insights from Anthropic's Survey

On March 18, 2026, Anthropic released the largest public survey on AI usage ever conducted. They asked 81,000 people across 40+ countries what they want—and don't want—from AI assistants.

The results are eye-opening.

In this article, we'll break down the key findings, explore what they mean for the future of AI, and show how OpenClaw is designed to address these real user needs.

---

## The Survey at a Glance

**Scale**: 81,000 participants  
**Countries**: 40+  
**Methodology**: Online surveys + in-depth interviews  
**Demographics**: Ages 18-65, various professions  

The survey covered three main areas:

1. **Desired AI capabilities** - What people want AI to do
2. **Concerns and risks** - What worries people about AI
3. **Use patterns** - How people currently use (or don't use) AI

---

## Top 10 Most Wanted AI Capabilities

Here's what users said they want most from AI assistants:

| Rank | Capability | % Wanting This |
|------|------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Memory & Personalization** | 78% |
| 2 | **Task Execution** | 74% |
| 3 | **Code Assistance** | 71% |
| 4 | **Research & Summarization** | 69% |
| 5 | **Writing Help** | 67% |
| 6 | **Learning & Tutoring** | 65% |
| 7 | **Data Analysis** | 62% |
| 8 | **Creative Brainstorming** | 58% |
| 9 | **Scheduling & Organization** | 54% |
| 10 | **Translation** | 51% |

### Key Insight: Memory is #1

The #1 requested feature was **memory and personalization**. Users want AI that:

- Remembers their preferences
- Understands their context
- Doesn't ask the same questions repeatedly
- Builds a relationship over time

This is exactly what OpenClaw's **memory system** provides. Unlike cloud AI services that start fresh each session, OpenClaw maintains persistent memory across conversations.

## Top Concerns About AI

Users also shared their biggest worries:

| Concern | % Worried |
|---------|-----------|
| **Privacy & Data Security** | 82% |
| **Accuracy & Hallucinations** | 76% |
| **Cost** | 68% |
| **Complexity** | 61% |
| **Job Displacement** | 54% |
| **Dependency** | 49% |

### Privacy is the Top Concern

82% of respondents worried about privacy. This includes:

- Data being used to train models without consent
- Sensitive information being leaked
- Lack of control over personal data
- Third-party access to conversations

OpenClaw is **self-hosted**. Your data never leaves your infrastructure:

## Generational Differences

The survey revealed interesting generational patterns:

### Gen Z (18-24)
- **Highest adoption**: 89% use AI regularly
- **Primary use**: Learning, creative work
- **Key concern**: Cost

### Millennials (25-40)
- **Strong adoption**: 78% use AI regularly
- **Primary use**: Work productivity, coding
- **Key concern**: Privacy

### Gen X (41-56)
- **Moderate adoption**: 54% use AI regularly
- **Primary use**: Research, data analysis
- **Key concern**: Accuracy

### Boomers (57-65)
- **Lower adoption**: 31% use AI regularly
- **Primary use**: Translation, writing help
- **Key concern**: Complexity

### Implications for AI Design

AI tools need to serve different needs:

- **Gen Z**: Affordable, creative, easy to use
- **Millennials**: Private, powerful, work-focused
- **Gen X**: Accurate, reliable, data-driven
- **Boomers**: Simple, helpful, well-documented

OpenClaw serves all these needs through:

- **Self-hosting** (privacy for Millennials)
- **Flexible model choice** (cost control for Gen Z)
- **Verified outputs** (accuracy for Gen X)
- **Simple setup** (accessibility for Boomers)

## What's Missing from Current AI Tools?

The survey also asked: "What frustrates you most about current AI tools?"

### Top Frustrations

1. **No memory between sessions** (71%)
   - Users hate repeating context
   - Want AI to "know" them

2. **Privacy concerns** (68%)
   - Don't trust cloud providers
   - Want local control

3. **Cost unpredictability** (64%)
   - Usage-based pricing is confusing
   - Unexpected bills

4. **Limited integration** (59%)
   - Can't connect to their tools
   - Isolated experience

5. **Hallucinations** (56%)
   - AI makes up facts
   - Can't verify sources
